## Hijama (Cupping Therapy)

Hijama is an ancient detoxification therapy that uses suction cups to draw out toxins and improve circulation. Benefits include:

- Pain relief
- Improved blood circulation
- Detoxification
- Reduced inflammation
- Enhanced immune function
- Stress relief

### Types of Cupping

- **Dry Cupping**: Suction only
- **Wet Cupping**: Suction with minor skin pricks for detoxification
- **Fire Cupping**: Traditional heated cup method
