Maintaining a healthy diet in Bali requires understanding local foods and balancing traditional wisdom with modern nutrition principles. Here's your guide to eating well on the island.

## Balinese Diet Basics

### Local Foods
- Fresh tropical fruits
- Abundant vegetables
- Fresh seafood
- Rice as staple
- Traditional spices

### Nutritional Benefits
- High in antioxidants
- Rich in vitamins
- Natural ingredients
- Fresh preparation
- Minimal processing

## Building Your Plate

### Macronutrient Balance

**Proteins (25-30%)**
- Fresh fish
- Chicken
- Tofu and tempeh
- Eggs
- Legumes

**Carbohydrates (45-50%)**
- Brown rice
- Sweet potatoes
- Fruits
- Vegetables
- Whole grains

**Healthy Fats (20-25%)**
- Coconut oil
- Avocado
- Nuts and seeds
- Olive oil
- Fish oils

### Micronutrients

**Vitamins**
- Vitamin A: Carrots, sweet potatoes
- Vitamin C: Citrus, papaya
- Vitamin D: Sun exposure, fish
- B Vitamins: Whole grains, meats

**Minerals**
- Calcium: Leafy greens, fish
- Iron: Red meat, spinach
- Zinc: Nuts, seeds, meats
- Magnesium: Dark chocolate, nuts

- Variety of nutrients

**Gado-gado**

- High in omega-3
- Lean protein

**High Sodium**
- Processed foods
- Some sauces
- Preserved items
