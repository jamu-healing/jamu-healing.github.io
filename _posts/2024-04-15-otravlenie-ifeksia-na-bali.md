Food poisoning and infections are common concerns for visitors to Bali. Understanding prevention and knowing how to respond can help you stay healthy during your stay.

## Common Causes

### Food-Related Issues
- Contaminated water
- Undercooked food
- Street food hygiene
- Improper food storage

### Environmental Factors
- Bacteria in local water
- Different food preparation methods
- Climate adjustment
- Travel stress

## Prevention Strategies

### Food Safety
- Drink bottled or filtered water
- Eat at reputable establishments
- Avoid raw foods initially
- Wash fruits thoroughly

### Personal Hygiene
- Regular hand washing
- Carry hand sanitizer
- Use clean utensils
- Avoid touching face

## Treatment Approaches

### Immediate Response
- Rest and hydration
- Avoid solid foods temporarily
- Monitor symptoms
- Seek medical help if severe

### Traditional Remedies
- Ginger tea for nausea
- Activated charcoal
- Electrolyte solutions
- Herbal preparations

### When to Seek Help
- High fever
- Severe dehydration
- Bloody stools
- Symptoms lasting more than 24 hours
