Mango is not just a delicious tropical fruit - it holds significant value in traditional Javanese medicine. At Jamu Healing, we incorporate natural foods like mango into our holistic wellness approach.

## Traditional Uses of Mango

In Javanese traditional medicine, mango has been used for centuries for its therapeutic properties:

### Digestive Support
- Promotes healthy digestion
- Relieves constipation naturally
- Supports gut health and balance

### Nutritional Benefits
- Rich in vitamins A and C
- Contains antioxidants
- Supports immune function
- Promotes healthy skin

### Cooling Properties
- Helps balance body heat
- Refreshing during hot weather
- Supports hydration
