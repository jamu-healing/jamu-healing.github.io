Kinesio taping is a valuable tool in our treatment arsenal at Jamu Healing. This case study demonstrates how we combine taping with osteopathic care for optimal results.

## Case Presentation

### Patient Profile
- Age: 35 years old
- Occupation: Office worker
- Complaint: Chronic shoulder pain
- Duration: 6 months

### Initial Assessment
- Limited shoulder range of motion
- Muscle tension in upper back
- Poor posture
- Pain with daily activities

## Treatment Approach

### Osteopathic Assessment
- Spinal alignment check
- Shoulder joint evaluation
- Muscle balance analysis
- Posture assessment

### Kinesio Taping Application

**Purpose:**
- Pain reduction
- Muscle support
- Improved circulation
- Enhanced proprioception

**Technique:**
- Y-strip for muscle support
- I-strip for ligament support
- Corrective application for posture
- Lymphatic drainage strips

## Treatment Protocol

### Session 1
- Initial assessment
- First taping application
- Home exercise instructions
- Follow-up schedule

### Session 2 (1 week later)
- Progress evaluation
- Tape replacement
- Exercise progression
- Additional treatment

### Session 3 (2 weeks later)
- Final assessment
- Maintenance taping
- Long-term exercise plan
- Prevention strategies

## Results

### Immediate Effects
- Pain reduction: 60%
- Improved mobility
- Better posture awareness
- Increased comfort

### Long-term Outcomes
- Sustained pain relief
- Return to normal activities
- Improved work ergonomics
- Better quality of life

## Key Learnings

### Integration Benefits
- Osteopathy addresses root cause
- Taping provides support
- Combined approach more effective
- Patient compliance improved

### Patient Education
- Proper taping technique
- Home care instructions
- Prevention strategies
- When to seek help

## Applications

Kinesio taping is effective for:

- Sports injuries

- Rehabilitation
- Prevention
