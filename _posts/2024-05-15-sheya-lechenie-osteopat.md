Neck pain is one of the most common complaints we treat at Jamu Healing. Understanding the causes and proper treatment approaches can help you find lasting relief.

## Common Causes of Neck Pain

### Structural Issues
- Poor posture
- Spinal misalignment
- Disc problems
- Muscle imbalances

### Lifestyle Factors
- Desk work
- Phone usage
- Sleeping position
- Stress and tension

### Medical Conditions
- Arthritis
- Injuries
- Infections
- Nerve compression

### Daily Habits
- Proper posture awareness
- Regular stretching
- Ergonomic setup

## When to Seek Help

Consult a professional if you experience:

- Persistent pain
- Radiating symptoms
- Numbness or tingling
- Headaches
- Limited mobility
