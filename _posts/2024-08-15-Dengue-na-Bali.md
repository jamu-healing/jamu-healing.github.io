Dengue fever is a concern for anyone living in or visiting Bali. Understanding prevention and knowing how to support recovery can help you stay healthy.

## Understanding Dengue

### What is Dengue?
- Mosquito-borne viral infection
- Common in tropical regions
- Seasonal outbreaks occur
- No specific treatment

### Transmission
- Aedes mosquitoes
- Daytime biting
- Urban environments
- Standing water

## Symptoms

### Initial Signs
- High fever (40°C/104°F)
- Severe headache
- Eye pain
- Muscle and joint pain
- Nausea and vomiting

### Warning Signs
- Severe abdominal pain
- Persistent vomiting
- Rapid breathing
- Bleeding gums
- Fatigue and restlessness

## Prevention Strategies

### Personal Protection
- Use mosquito repellent
- Wear long sleeves
- Sleep under nets
- Avoid peak hours

### Environmental Control
- Eliminate standing water
- Keep surroundings clean
- Use screens
- Regular fogging

### During Outbreaks
- Stay indoors during peak times
- Use air conditioning
- Cover water containers
- Community cleanup

## Medical Treatment

### Conventional Care
- Hydration
- Pain management
- Platelet monitoring
- Hospitalization if severe

### When to Seek Help
- Fever above 39°C
- Severe pain
- Bleeding
- Difficulty breathing
- Persistent vomiting

### Nutritional Support

### Rest and Recovery
- Complete bed rest
- Gradual activity increase
- Follow-up care

## Recovery Process

### Timeline
- Acute phase: 3-7 days
- Recovery: 1-2 weeks
- Full recovery: 4-6 weeks

### Support Measures
- Adequate rest
- Nutritious diet
- Hydration
- Gentle movement

## Complications

### Warning Signs
- Dengue hemorrhagic fever
- Dengue shock syndrome
- Organ involvement
- Secondary infections

### Prevention
- Early detection
- Proper treatment
- Follow-up care
- Monitoring
