## Meet Evi Sudarto

Evi Sudarto is a highly skilled osteopath and manual therapist with over 30 years of extensive experience in diagnosing and treating musculoskeletal disorders. As a creator of a unique therapeutic methodology, Evi combines modern osteopathic techniques with traditional Javanese medicine to provide holistic healing solutions.

## Our Mission

At Jamu Healing, we believe in the power of natural medicine and traditional healing practices. Our mission is to bring the ancient wisdom of Javanese medicine to modern wellness, helping people achieve optimal health through:

- **Natural Healing**: Using time-tested herbal remedies and manual therapies
- **Holistic Approach**: Treating the whole person, not just symptoms
- **Personalized Care**: Tailoring treatments to individual needs
- **Education**: Empowering patients with knowledge about their health

## Experience & Expertise

With three decades of practice, Evi has developed expertise in:

- **Osteopathy**: Manual therapy for musculoskeletal issues
- **Traditional Javanese Medicine**: Herbal remedies and natural treatments
- **Chiropractic Care**: Spinal adjustment and alignment
- **Visceral Therapy**: Internal organ manipulation
- **Postnatal Care**: Recovery support after childbirth
- **Hijama (Cupping)**: Traditional detoxification therapy

## Unique Methodology

Evi's approach integrates:

1. **Modern Osteopathy**: Evidence-based manual therapy techniques
2. **Traditional Jamu**: Herbal medicine from Java
3. **Holistic Assessment**: Understanding the root cause of issues
4. **Preventive Care**: Maintaining health before problems arise
