# Welcome to Jamu Healing

## About Us
Jamu Healing is dedicated to the art and science of traditional Indonesian herbal medicine known as Jamu. Our mission is to promote health and wellness through 100% natural remedies made from local ingredients that have been used for centuries.

## Our Services
- **Consultations**: Personalized wellness plans and consultations to meet your health needs.
- **Workshops**: Interactive sessions where you learn how to prepare your own Jamu medicine.
- **Product Sales**: A range of carefully crafted Jamu products available for purchase directly from our site.

## Ingredients
Our Jamu is made using a variety of ingredients, including but not limited to:
- Turmeric
- Ginger
- Galangal
- Tamarind
- Honey
- Lemongrass

Each ingredient is chosen for its unique health benefits as well as its role in traditional practices.
